## Why Privacy Matters

Most online converters upload your files to their servers:
- Your files exist on someone else's computer
- Data may be stored, analyzed, or leaked
- Sensitive files (contracts, personal audio) at risk

**Client-side converters like [MixConvert](https://mixconvert.com) process files locally:**
- Files never leave your device
- Works offline
- No data collection
